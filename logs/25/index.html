1 pixel scroll demo with colored border for performance assessment

#msx #msx2 #gradius #gamedev #demo #shmup #sidescroller #retrogamedev

red = blitter
yellow = controls and sprites
blue = music

It is important to keep the blitter (red) and processing of sprites (yellow) out of the visible screen when rasterizing (rendering the screen) if you want to see them without flickering. This little interval is called the vertical blank or vblank for short and it's when video operations are the fastest. To squeeze the maximum amount of performance of the MSX2, I am using line interrupts around 181 (this value is obtained experimentally so it may change) instead of waiting for vblanks and I also turn off the screen at the end of screen rasterization and turn it back on at the beginning because blitting operations are faster this way.
