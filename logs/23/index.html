hoje estava olhando uns vídeos de Windows, e notei que muita gente nem sabe porqué CR+LF vs LF, então aqui tem um resumo da história:
Ismael Luceno
TTYs use CR+LF behind scenes (and emulation does that too); in fact, real TTYs often require extra time to reposition the head in addition to the sequence (often handled by sending NULs); this is one of the reasons that prompted Multics to have a streamlined I/O filter framework (idea inherited by Unix), which also made adding extra features easy; using just LF represents a saving of 2.5%-3% depending on the nature of the file (based on modern UNIX code and documentation files), and storage was extremely expensive back then, plus these were research products. LF was chosen over CR because in practice CR was also used to fake a boldface in real TTYs. Windows inherited CR+LF usage from DEC, indirectly, through DOS, for compatibility with CP/M. DEC systems were dumb, and CP/M was heavily influenced by DEC's TOPS-10 in all aspects because Gary Kildall used it for development and thus wanted compatibility.
